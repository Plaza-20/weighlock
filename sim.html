Simulation notes: every figure comes from the inputs you enter. Load-cell noise and vibration shake are absolute errors in kg that you set (0 gives a perfect sensor), and the 15-sample average removes most of the shake. Tolerance is a percentage of the expected net weight. A truck whose swapped load weighs within the tolerance window of what was removed passes the weight check, which is why the full design adds camera snapshots and pallet-level weighing. Exact counting also needs the total weighing error to stay under half a unit weight, so light units on a truck scale are far harder to count than heavy ones.
